# Band Ratioing Definition
Band ratioing is a technique used in Remote Sensing to effectively display spectral variations. 
Spectral band ratioing enhances the desired compositional information while suppressing other types of information, such as the terrain slope and grain size differences.
# Different Indices
## NDVI :
The NDVI is a band ratioing involving visible red and near-infrared bands of satellite images and determines the vegetation cover over a particular area.
